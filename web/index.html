How it works

  1. Press New model, pick a world and find the place on the map.
  2. Frame it with the circle, or draw your own contour, and capture it.
  3. Set the size, the heights and the look; save it, or export it for printing.

On the map, Two places side by side puts two places of any worlds on one round model, at the same scale: a crater on the Moon next to Elbrus.

    Craft on the surface

    The circle's width follows the zoom of the map: it is written on the circle.

    The edge follows the nearest ridge or valley around the circle, at most a fifth of its radius in or out. It is found again when the map stops.

    Click the corners on the map. A click on the first corner, a double click or Enter closes the contour. Drag a corner to move it.

    Cmd+Z or Ctrl+Z takes the last point back; with Shift it comes back.

    The ruler: carry its ring to another point. Its foot keeps the level of the “0”; carry the “0” for another level. Esc puts the ruler away.

    Speeds

    Drag to look. W and S fly forward and back, A and D turn, Q and E slide, Shift goes up and Ctrl down, R and F tilt the view and Space levels it. Two short quick taps of a key hold its move on; one more press lets it go. + and − change the speed, Alt held is slower, the wheel glides. On a phone: one finger looks, two fingers pinch and slide.
